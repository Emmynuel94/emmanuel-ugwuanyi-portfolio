Consulting project HR analytics SQL · Excel · Power BI

Employee Retention & Performance Analytics

Objective
Analyse employee data to understand workforce performance, employee turnover and compensation structures. The goal was to uncover the causes of attrition, evaluate departmental performance and assess salary fairness, so HR and leadership could improve retention, performance management and workforce planning.
My contribution
I analysed the employee data for trends affecting retention, departmental performance and salary distribution. I cleaned and validated the datasets, ran exploratory analysis, investigated turnover, compared performance across departments and assessed compensation patterns. I used SQL to analyse the data, built visualisations and dashboards, and presented recommendations to support HR decisions.
Tools & approach
  • SQL
  • Microsoft Excel
  • Power BI
Data cleaning and validation in Excel, extraction and querying in SQL, exploratory data analysis, attrition analysis, departmental performance evaluation, salary analysis, KPI development and dashboard creation.

Outcome & key findings

Delivered a SQL-based HR analytics project for a client, turning employee data into insights to support workforce planning and management decisions.

  • Engineering and Marketing recorded the highest employee turnover, with 9 departures each.
  • Sales had the lowest average performance score (4.00).
  • Departmental salary averages differed, and the employees with the highest absence levels were highlighted.
  • SQL queries extracted, joined and aggregated data across multiple HR tables.

Consulting project Sales analytics Power BI · DAX

Novamed Solution: Sales Performance Dashboard

Objective
Build an interactive sales performance dashboard covering revenue, profit, profit margin, quantity sold and cost of goods sold (COGS). It needed to track monthly performance, identify top and bottom performing drugs and customers, and show their contribution to overall sales.
My contribution
I analysed the sales data to evaluate overall performance and calculated the key metrics. I built month-on-month comparisons for revenue and profit, identified the top five and bottom five drugs and customers and assessed their contribution to total sales. I then built the interactive Power BI dashboard with dynamic visuals and slicers so users can explore the data themselves.
Tools & approach
  • Power BI
  • Power Query
  • DAX
  • Microsoft Excel
Data cleaning and transformation, data modelling, KPI development, profit margin calculation, month-on-month analysis, top and bottom ranking, percentage contribution analysis, customer segmentation and slicer-based filtering.

Outcome

Delivered an interactive Power BI dashboard giving a consolidated view of revenue, profit, profit margins, COGS and sales volume.

  • Enabled month-by-month performance comparison.
  • Identified top and bottom performing drugs and customers and their share of overall sales.
  • Improved visibility of sales performance, supporting decisions on growth opportunities and underperforming areas.
